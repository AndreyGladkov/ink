Ink Tasks is a to-do list in the manner of Google Tasks for Android phones with e-ink screens, built for the Hisense A9 as a companion to the other Ink apps. Tasks are kept in plain Markdown files in the format of the Obsidian Tasks plugin.

Every pixel is pure black or white, with no antialiasing and no animations.

<ul>
<li>Several lists, tasks with notes, due dates and subtasks, done tasks on a page of their own.</li>
<li>Due dates by Today, Tomorrow, In a week or a month calendar; overdue dates stand out white on black.</li>
<li>Reminders at a time on the due date, with Done and In an hour in the notification; reminders set in Obsidian with the Reminder plugin ring too.</li>
<li>Tasks are moved by tapping marks between them, with no dragging; deleting asks for a second tap instead of a dialog.</li>
<li>Kept on the phone, or in a folder you choose, such as one in an Obsidian vault, to sync with a computer.</li>
<li>Only the lines a change is about are rewritten; everything else in the file stays as it is.</li>
</ul>

The app does not use the internet; it asks only to show notifications, for reminders.
