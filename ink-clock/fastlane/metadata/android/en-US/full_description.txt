Ink Clock is a clock app for Android phones with e-ink screens, built for the Hisense A9 as a companion to Ink Phone and Ink Messages.

Every pixel is pure black or white, with no antialiasing and no animations. Nothing redraws faster than once a minute except the stopwatch while it is on screen and the last minute of a timer.

<ul>
<li>Alarms with repeat days, labels, ringtones and vibration; they ring over the lock screen, even before the first unlock after a reboot. Snooze is the big button, Dismiss is held for a second.</li>
<li>Timer typed on a keypad, with recent durations, pause and +1 min.</li>
<li>Stopwatch with laps.</li>
<li>Pomodoro drawn as a big tomato that work eats and a break fills back, one minute at a time, with a daily goal.</li>
<li>Desk clock and a charging screensaver with the time, the date and the next alarm, or the pomodoro under way.</li>
<li>Other apps can set alarms and timers through Android's standard alarm intents.</li>
</ul>

The app has no internet access.
