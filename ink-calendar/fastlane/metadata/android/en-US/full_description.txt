Ink Calendar is a calendar for Android phones with e-ink screens, laid out like the month view of the iPhone's Calendar, built for the Hisense A9 as a companion to the other Ink apps. It shows the calendars already on the phone, so whatever your Google or CalDAV account syncs is there.

Every pixel is pure black or white, with no antialiasing and no animations.

<ul>
<li>The month on top, the chosen day's events below; today filled, the chosen day ringed, a dot under every day with events.</li>
<li>Whole months turn with arrows or a swipe, with no scrolling; the month's title opens the year.</li>
<li>Events with a time, a calendar, a place, notes and reminders; adding, changing and deleting them, with a second tap instead of a dialog.</li>
<li>Reminders as notifications with In 10 minutes and Close.</li>
<li>A home screen widget with the date and what is left of today and tomorrow, redrawn only when that changes.</li>
<li>Any calendar can be hidden; with no account calendar, events stay in a calendar on the phone.</li>
</ul>

The app does not use the internet; it asks for access to the phone's calendars and to show notifications, for reminders.
